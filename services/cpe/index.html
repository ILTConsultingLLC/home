Understanding Accessibility

The most sustainable way to ensure accessibility is to build a culture of inclusion within your team. We provide Continuing Professional Education (CPE) and specialized training sessions designed for educators, digital content creators, and makerspace coordinators.

Drawing on research and practical experience within the field of special education we deliver high-impact learning experiences that translate complex compliance requirements and pedagogical theories into actionable, everyday skills for your staff.

Sessions

  • Document Remediation Workshops: Hands-on training for staff to create accessible PDFs, Word documents, and presentations in-house.
  • Inclusive Maker Pedagogy: Professional development sessions for educators on supporting students with severe disabilities in STEM/STEAM environments.
  • Tactile Teachables Design Seminars: Training on the principles of haptic learning and 3D-printed tactile graphics.
  • Custom CPE Packages: Tailored curriculum designed to meet your organization’s specific professional certification or compliance needs in the field of special education.
  • Disability Ethics & Digital Equity: Keynote sessions on the intersection of technology, research, and the lived experience of disability.

Our Approach

Our educational philosophy is rooted in practical application. We move beyond “checkbox compliance” to show the why behind inclusive design, using real-world case studies from research and professional practice. Whether delivered virtually or on-site, each session is interactive, accessible, and structured to provide participants with immediate tools and frameworks they can apply to their work the very next day.
