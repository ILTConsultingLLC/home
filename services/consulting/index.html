Workflow Automation

Google Apps Script is a powerful, cloud-based platform that allows us to build custom tools and automate repetitive tasks directly within your Google Workspace. We write specialized code that connects your favorite tools like Sheets, Docs, and Gmail to create seamless digital environments that increase accuracy and efficiency in your workflow.

What We Offer

  • Custom Web Applications: We build standalone web applications or tools embedded directly into your Google Sites to manage internal school processes.

  • Sheet Enhancement: We develop custom functions and macros for Google Sheets to automate repetitive tasks, reduce human error, and extend the capabilities of standard spreadsheet formulas.

  • Automated Communication: We create scripts that act like “mini-bots” who handle repetitive tasks like sending personalized emails, notifications, or reports through Gmail based on triggers in your Google Forms or Sheets.

  • Custom Interface Tools: To simplify data entry for staff, we add custom menus, sidebars, and dialog boxes directly into the Docs and Sheets your team already uses.

  • Tailored Add-ons: We develop and deploy lightweight add-ons specifically designed for your institution’s unique administrative needs.

Our Approach

We take a collaborative approach, working alongside your team to ensure that our solutions are practical, achievable, and aligned with your goals. Every engagement begins with a thorough discovery phase to understand your current state and desired outcomes. We will then leverage Google’s rapid development environment to build your solutions quickly and securely. Because Apps Script runs on Google’s own servers and saves directly to your Google Drive, there is never any software for your IT department to install or maintain. Our process focuses on using modern, web-standard technology to bridge the gaps between Google Workspace applications, ensuring that your school’s workflow is as efficient, integrated, and user-friendly as possible.
