Implementation & Coaching

Turning a vision into reality is a journey we take together. Drawing on the Impact Cycle and Energy Leadership coaching frameworks, we provide the partnership-driven support your team needs to move from “knowing” to “doing” with confidence and clarity.

What We Offer

  • Coaching Cycles: We utilize Jim Knight’s Impact Cycle (Identify, Learn, Improve) to help staff master new accessibility tools and automated workflows through a proven, peer-to-peer process.
  • Energy Leadership & Growth: We apply iPEC principles to help leaders and teams identify their “blocks” and shift their energy toward high-impact, sustainable solutions.
  • Mini-Coaching Sessions: Targeted, “just-in-time” support designed to solve specific technical or pedagogical hurdles without the burnout of long-form training.
  • GROWTH Framework Integration: We facilitate goal-setting and reality-checking to ensure that new technology integrations align perfectly with your school’s unique mission and culture.

Our Approach

Our philosophy is rooted in the belief that true change happens through partnership, not prescription. We don’t just “deliver” a project; we enter into a collaborative cycle with your team. By embedding as a coach and partner, we help you navigate the transition from current state to desired state, removing blockers and building internal capacity. We are here to listen, to model, and to support you until the new “way of doing” becomes second nature.
