SPED Funding Engine

Facilitate Tier Intensity and Service Group Classifications

The SPED Funding Engine is a web-based tool designed to streamline the process of classifying Tier Intensity and Service Groups for special education funding. It simplifies complex workflows so educators and administrators can focus on what matters most — supporting students.

Key Features

  • Streamlined classification of Tier Intensity levels
  • Service Group assignment support
  • Secure Google-based authentication
  • Built for school district administrators and special education staff

Access

The SPED Funding Engine is currently available to authorized district accounts. If your organization is interested in purchasing access, contact us .
