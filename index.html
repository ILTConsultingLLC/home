Pragmatic Solutions for Complex Work

Our mission is to ensure that innovation never leaves anyone behind.

Interdisciplinary Learning and Teaching

Our process addresses complex pedagogical problems by merging research-driven strategies with technical ingenuity. Whether we are streamlining administrative workflows or building custom classroom tools, our process redefines energy sucking obstacles into efficient, tech-powered solutions that empower educators and leaders to focus on what matters most.

Bespoke Digital Tools and Support

We bridge the gap between abstract technology and practical classroom application through a diverse range of specialized services.

Workflow Automation

Google Apps Script is a powerful, cloud-based platform that allows us to build custom tools and automate repetitive tasks directly within your Google Workspace. We write specialized code that connects your favorite tools like Sheets, Docs, and Gmail to create seamless digital environments that increase accuracy and efficiency in your workflow.

Document Remediation

Inaccessible digital files aren’t just a barrier for users; they are a legal and reputational risk for your campus. We provide a meticulous, human-in-the-loop approach to document remediation.

Tactile Teachables

Accessibility goes beyond the screen. While document remediation makes text accessible to screen readers, abstract concepts like architectural layouts, biological cells, or complex geometric data can often be perceived more effectively through concrete objects.

Understanding Accessibility

The most sustainable way to ensure accessibility is to build a culture of inclusion within your team. We provide Continuing Professional Education (CPE) and specialized training sessions designed for educators, digital content creators, and makerspace coordinators.

Inclusive Makerspaces

True innovation requires a seat at the table for everyone. Drawing on research regarding makerspaces and students with severe disabilities, we provide specialized consulting to help schools, libraries, and community hubs transform their maker environments into truly inclusive spaces.

Implementation & Coaching

Turning a vision into reality is a journey we take together. Drawing on the Impact Cycle and Energy Leadership coaching frameworks, we provide the partnership-driven support your team needs to move from “knowing” to “doing” with confidence and clarity.
