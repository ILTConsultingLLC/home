About

Our Mission

At ILT Consulting, LLC, our mission is to ensure that innovation never leaves anyone behind. We empower educational institutions and organizations to build inclusive digital and physical environments through custom automation, meticulous document remediation, and partnership-driven coaching.

We believe that accessibility is not a checkbox; it is a culture of belonging that we build together.

The Intersection of Technology & Pedagogy

What sets us apart is our dual-lens approach. We don’t just provide technical solutions; we ensure those solutions are pedagogically sound and sustainable. Our work is grounded in:

  • The TPACK Framework: We operate at the intersection of Technological, Pedagogical, and Content Knowledge. We don’t just “add tech”; we ensure the technology serves the specific instructional content and the way students actually learn.
  • Universal Design for Learning (UDL): Our designs are rooted in the three pillars of UDL—Engagement, Representation, and Action/Expression. We build environments that provide multiple points of entry for every learner.
  • Constructionism & Maker Pedagogy: Following Piaget and Papert, we believe that learning happens best when students are “making” tangible objects. Our inclusive makerspace consulting ensures students with severe disabilities are active creators, not just observers.
  • The Partnership Principles: We utilize Jim Knight’s Impact Cycle to ensure our technical implementations result in actual student and staff growth through a peer-to-peer relationship.
  • Energy Leadership: We use iPEC principles to help your team navigate the “inner” blocks that often stall “outer” technical transitions.

Our Values

  • Partnership over Prescription — We sit side-by-side with your or your team as co-creators of your school’s success.
  • Dignified Accessibility — Whether it is a PDF or a 3D-printed floor plan, we believe every user deserves a clear, intuitive, and dignified experience.
  • Sustainable Innovation — We build customized tools (like Google Apps Script workflows) that save your team resources—specifically the most valuable one: time.
  • Human-First Implementation — Technology should serve the human experience. We measure our success not by the code we write, but by the barriers we remove for your students and staff.

Our Philosophy

We believe that when we design for the “margins,” we improve the experience for the “middle.” By leveraging technology, evidence-based research on exceptionality, and your content knowledge, we create innovative interdisciplinary solutions. Whether through workshops, coaching, or hands-on implementation, our goal is to build your internal capacity to sustain efficiency and success.
